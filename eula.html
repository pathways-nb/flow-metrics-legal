What the app does with your data

Described in full in our Privacy Policy. In short: FlowPaths is read-only, makes no external network calls, and requests no user-identity scope — it cannot read assignee, reporter, or any personal field. Configuration is stored in Atlassian’s own infrastructure; a per-viewer cache is held only in that viewer’s own browser and is never transmitted to us.

What the app is, and is not

FlowPaths is a decision-support tool built on your team’s historical data. Its percentiles and forecasts describe how work has flowed in the past and express future outcomes as probabilities. They are not guarantees of future results, and should not be treated as such.
